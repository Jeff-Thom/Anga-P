Problem

Manual rent tracking

Leads to missed payments and unclear records.

Fragmented communication

Tenant messages scattered across WhatsApp, SMS, and email.

Paper receipts

No digital audit trail for payments or leases.

Spreadsheet dependence

Error-prone and difficult to scale across units.

Maintenance delays

Requests get lost, contractors are delayed, tenants are unhappy.

Solution

RealEstOs replaces scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and paper trails with a single, intelligent dashboard. Automated rent collection, smart reminders, and structured maintenance workflows help landlords recover time, reduce missed payments, and keep complete records — without hiring extra staff.

How AI Works

Powered by Gemini. RealEstOs uses practical AI to handle the busywork that wastes hours every week.

Understands messages

Summarizes tenant conversations automatically.

Drafts reminders

Generates polite, contextual payment follow-ups.

Extracts receipt data

Reads payment receipts and logs key details.

Categorizes maintenance

Tags requests by type and urgency.

Generates reports

Produces cashflow and occupancy summaries.
