The Problem

High-friction workflows cost landlords time and money.

Manual rent tracking

Leads to missed payments and unclear records.

Fragmented communication

Tenant messages scattered across WhatsApp, SMS, and email.

Paper receipts

No digital audit trail for payments or leases.

Spreadsheet dependence

Error-prone and hard to scale across units.

Maintenance delays

Requests get lost, contractors are delayed, tenants are unhappy.

Solution

RealEstOs replaces scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and paper trails with one intelligent dashboard. Automated rent collection, smart reminders, and structured maintenance workflows help landlords recover time, reduce missed payments, and keep complete records.
